Growth beyond word of mouth.

Great Fold Group’s founder and CEO, Jason Parnass, spent more than a decade in food service, in corporate kitchens and in mom-and-pop shops. In both, Jason kept running into the same barrier to growth, and it was one the businesses put up themselves: smaller operations weren’t using marketing at all, not in the free channels they already owned and not in paid ones.

Instead, they counted on word of mouth to bring people through the door. Word of mouth is great when it’s working, but it leaves a business riding every slow week and busy season with nothing else pulling in new customers.

Jason has also spent more than a decade in consumer insights, digital analytics, and market research, leading 100+ studies for brands like Disney, Microsoft, and Starbucks. Big brands rarely guess. They survey their customers, test their prices, and track what every marketing dollar brings back. Great Fold Group was founded to bring that same toolkit to small businesses and help them grow beyond their word-of-mouth circle.

Today we work with owners who are great at what they do and ready for steadier growth. We keep it small on purpose: you work directly with Jason, from the first call to the final results. No handoffs, no jargon, just clear answers sized to your budget and a plan you can act on.

Why small business

You don’t need a big-brand budget. You need the right plan.

Marketing usually isn’t the top priority for a small business. You’re focused on keeping the lights on and the bills paid. But if you’re not putting your brand in front of people, you’ll end up on the struggle bus.

Marketing isn’t one size fits all, and the goal isn’t to copy what big companies spend. With small optimizations and a clear strategy, any small business can see real results, from social media posts to paid ads.

Why “Great Fold”?

It comes from poker.

If you want a winning session, you need a great fold strategy: knowing what you have, admitting what you don’t know yet, and understanding the risk on both sides. Marketing and growth can feel like a game full of missing information. Our job is to help you play it well.
